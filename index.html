Capital Available for Deployment

Capital available for deployment is the total pool of funds Karandaaz can invest at the end of a period. It comprises funds drawn under EAGR and SEED, external grant funds for NCGCL, RDEL and own source revenue (OSR) funds injected for Women Ventures, OSR funds available with Treasury, and the liquidity reserve held against the outstanding portfolio.
